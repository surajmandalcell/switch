About Switch

Change coding accounts without signing in again. Save logins for supported tools, check usage, and choose an account from your Mac's menu bar.

Free and open source.

Switch is free under the MIT license. The Mac app and the command-line tool share the same account and recovery features. The command-line tool also runs on Linux.

It is made by Suraj Mandal.
