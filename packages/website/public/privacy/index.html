Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Switch is a free, open-source Mac app made by Suraj Mandal ("we"). This policy covers the Switch app, its command-line tool, and this website. In short, Switch does not collect your data.

The short version

  • There is no account to create, and no server that stores your data.
  • There are no analytics, telemetry, ads, or trackers, in the app or on this website.
  • This website sets no cookies.
  • Your logins stay in private files on your Mac. Switch never reads the macOS Keychain or browser cookie stores.

What the app keeps on your Mac

Switch keeps everything it needs on your Mac, in files that only your macOS user account can read:

  • The login files of each account you add, each in its own private folder.
  • A backup of the old login, saved before every switch, so you can always go back.
  • Your settings.

Insights reads the usage logs that your AI tools already keep on your Mac, and the usage charts that some services provide. It shows the results only to you.

None of this data is sent to us. To remove an account and its saved login, remove the account in the app.

When the app uses the internet

Switch has no server of its own. It connects only to these places, directly from your Mac:

  • Your AI services. To show usage limits, Switch asks each service you added for its usage, with your own login. For example, it asks Anthropic about a Claude Code account and OpenAI about a Codex account. It refreshes about every three minutes, and when you ask.
  • Status pages. Switch can check the public status page of each service, such as status.claude.com, to show outages. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • A public price list. To estimate API cost in Insights, Switch downloads a public list of model prices from GitHub. This request contains no account details.

Each AI service handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Switch never sends your logins, email addresses, or usage to us or to anyone else.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and loads no fonts or scripts from other sites.
  • Railway hosts the site and Cloudflare delivers it. To serve and protect the pages, they process your IP address and basic request details, such as your browser type and the page you asked for. We do not use this data to identify or track you.
  • Downloads and source code are on GitHub. When you download Switch or open a GitHub link, GitHub handles the request under the GitHub Privacy Statement. To show the newest version number, the site can also ask GitHub's public API for the latest release, straight from your browser.
  • If you run the command-line tool with npx, npm delivers it under the npm privacy policy.

Children

Switch is a tool for software developers. It is not made for children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Your choices

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to show, correct, or delete. The data that Switch uses is on your Mac and under your control. To remove it, remove your accounts in the app, then delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top. The full history of this page is public on GitHub.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on GitHub. To report a security problem, follow the steps on the Security page.
